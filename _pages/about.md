      <p class="eyebrow">RESEARCH PROJECT · ACCEPTED AT COLM 2026</p>
      <h2>SciTaRC</h2>
      <p>Scientific Tabular Reasoning and Claims</p>
    </header>

    <div class="research-overview reveal" id="research-overview">
      <div>
        <p class="research-index">Overview</p>
        <p>SciTaRC is an expert-authored benchmark that asks whether AI systems can understand scientific tables, form the right plan, and execute it faithfully.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <p>The study separates comprehension, planning, and execution. Its central result is precise: even leading reasoning models often know what to do, then fail to carry it out correctly.</p>
        <div class="research-links"><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08910">Paper ↗</a><a href="https://github.com/JHU-CLSP/SciTaRC">Code ↗</a><a href="https://huggingface.co/datasets/JHU-CLSP/SciTaRC">Dataset ↗</a></div>
      </div>
